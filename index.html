Privacy Policy

This policy covers the internal ClearPathLT marketing application, including its Pinterest API integration. It does not replace the privacy policies of Pinterest, Etsy, GitHub, or other service providers.

Who operates this application

The application is operated by the owner of the ClearPathLT Etsy shop to promote the shop's own digital spreadsheet products. It is an internal tool, not a public service for managing unrelated users' Pinterest accounts.

Information the integration accesses

When the shop owner authorizes Pinterest access, the application processes the account information needed to identify the authorized account, its own board and Pin identifiers, titles, descriptions, image references, destination links, and relevant API responses. It also handles the original product images, promotional copy, and Etsy listing URLs selected for publication.

OAuth access and refresh tokens are used to make authorized API requests. The application does not ask for a Pinterest password. It is not intended to access other people's private boards, collect customer payment details, or manage advertising spending.

How information is used

Information is used to identify the selected board, prevent duplicate posts, prepare and publish original promotional Pins, update the owner's descriptions, and verify or troubleshoot those operations. Pinterest permissions are requested for these purposes, not for unrelated use.

AI processing and service providers

The tool uses Hermes Agent for AI-assisted marketing. Selected product content and Pinterest data needed for a task may be processed by the configured AI service provider. Do not provide sensitive information in Pin descriptions or other content used by the assistant. OAuth secrets should not be included in AI prompts or public files.

Authorized API requests are sent to Pinterest. Product links lead to Etsy. This policy page is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process ordinary website request information under GitHub's own privacy statement. Hosting this page does not require uploading the application's tokens or private data to GitHub. Information is not sold or shared for third-party advertising.

Storage, security, and retention

Operational records may include publication manifests, Pin identifiers, destination links, and diagnostic logs. Credentials must be kept separately from public website files and promotional assets, with access limited to the authorized operator. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Credentials and operational records should be retained only as needed for the authorized integration, troubleshooting, and legitimate recordkeeping, and deleted when no longer needed. Revoking authorization stops future authorized access but does not automatically remove existing published Pins, local logs, backups, or records retained by Pinterest or other providers.

Your choices and deletion requests

The account owner can revoke this application's access in Pinterest's connected-app settings. To request deletion of application-held data or ask a privacy question, contact the ClearPathLT owner through the Etsy shop's contact / Message seller option. Requests should identify the relevant account without including passwords or access tokens. Any stored copies and provider-held records must be handled according to their applicable controls and obligations.

Children

This internal business tool is not directed to children and is not intended to collect children's personal information.

Changes

This page may be updated if the application's operation or service providers change. The operator should review the policy against the actual implementation before enabling production access.
